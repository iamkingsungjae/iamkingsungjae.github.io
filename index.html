About Me

I am an M.S. student in the Division of Computer Engineering at Hansung University and a member of PRISM Lab.

My research explores how AI can better understand and personalize user preferences, with a focus on natural language processing and multimodal learning.

More recently, I have been exploring LLMs and LLM-based agents for recommendation, moving beyond traditional ranking toward generative, conversational, and explainable recommendation.
